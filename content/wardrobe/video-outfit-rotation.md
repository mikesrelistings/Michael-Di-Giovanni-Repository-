# Video Outfit Rotation

Built from the "12 Combinações Que Nunca Falham" palette (12 shirt + pants pairings). Translated, then put in a filming order that:

- alternates a dark shirt and a light shirt every video,
- never repeats the same shirt or the same pants two videos in a row,
- loops cleanly from #12 back to #1.

Every look in the source uses a black belt and white sneakers.

## Rotation order

| # | Shirt | Pants | Shirt tone | On-camera note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Navy | Grey | Dark | Blends into Cobalt backgrounds. Film on location, not over branded graphics |
| 2 | Beige | Light blue | Light | Warm and approachable. Good for homeowner and seller topics |
| 3 | Olive green | Brown | Dark | Never on a green screen |
| 4 | Light blue | Navy | Light | Closest to the Seaglass brand color. Safe over Cobalt |
| 5 | Burgundy | Grey | Dark | Strong contrast on Cobalt. Good for market-news and warning topics |
| 6 | White | Brown | Light | Bright shirts can blow out on camera. Lower exposure a touch |
| 7 | Black | Khaki | Dark | Loses detail on dark backgrounds. Use a light or busy background |
| 8 | Grey | Navy | Light | Neutral authority look. Works anywhere |
| 9 | Brown | Khaki | Dark | Warm, casual. Good for property walk-throughs |
| 10 | White | Grey | Light | Same exposure note as #6 |
| 11 | Navy | White | Dark | Same Cobalt note as #1 |
| 12 | Light blue | Brown | Light | Safe over Cobalt. Loops back to #1 |

## What you need to own (to wear all 12)

The 12 looks only need 9 shirts and 6 pants:

- **Shirts (9):** navy, grey, beige, olive green, light blue, brown, white, burgundy, black
- **Pants (6):** grey, navy, light blue, brown, khaki, white

## Talking-head videos

If the frame only shows you from the waist up, only the shirt reads on camera. That means 9 distinct looks, not 12. Pants matter only for full-body, walking or property-tour shots.

## Matching the video template

The Short template (`content/open-real-estate/video/short.html`) uses a Cobalt (#050E3D) background. If you ever put yourself on top of that background, wear #2, #4, #5, #8 or #12. Navy and black shirts disappear into it.
