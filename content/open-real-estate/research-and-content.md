## 1. The story in one paragraph

On October 26, 2026, four brokerage groups announced **Open Real Estate**, a national listing data-sharing initiative: AGNT (parent of eXp Realty and NextHome), HomeServices of America, Keller Williams, and Real REMAX Group. An agent enters a listing once and it goes to member brokerages and is syndicated to MLSs, home search portals and other approved recipients. It is free for any brokerage to join, it is pitched as working alongside the MLS (not replacing it), and it is a direct answer to the private listing fight led by Compass. It is not live yet. The target is early 2027.

**Your angle (the one the source reels miss):** Chicago is ground zero for the private listing fight, the studies on whether private listings cost sellers money disagree, and Open Real Estate still allows participant-only listings. Your message as a homeowner advocate: **before you sign a listing agreement, ask who will see your home, and when.**

## 4. Compliance flags (route to counsel or Real compliance; this is not legal advice)

1. **Your brokerage is a founder.** Real REMAX Group is a founding member and a public company (REAX). Check Real's social media and marketing policy before posting about a company initiative. Make no predictions about the company or the stock. Disclose the affiliation on every piece (line included below).
2. **Pending litigation.** Zillow v. MRED and Compass is in arbitration and the Compass case is paused. Say "Zillow sued" and "Zillow alleges." Never say anyone "conspired."
3. **No boycott language.** Never say or imply "we won't show" or "we won't cooperate with" another brokerage's listings. Group-boycott statements are an antitrust risk.
4. **Disclosure line wording.** I could not verify the corporate link between Real Broker, LLC and Real REMAX Group, so the line reads: "I'm an agent with Real. Real REMAX Group is one of the four founders." Confirm with Real if you want it tighter.

## 9. Lead magnet: "Who Will See My Home?" (send to anyone who DMs EXPOSURE)

**Who Will See My Home? 5 questions to ask before you sign a listing agreement**

Where your home is marketed is your decision. These five questions make sure you are making it with the facts in front of you.

1. **Will my home go on the MLS, and on what date?** Get the date in writing.
2. **Will it show on Zillow, Realtor.com and Redfin?** Ask which sites, and when.
3. **If you recommend private or coming soon first, for how long, and what data backs that for my price range?** Ask for numbers, not a pitch. Zillow's 2025 study and Compass's own data point in opposite directions, and both companies have a stake.
4. **Could your brokerage end up representing the buyer too?** Ask how that works and what you would be asked to sign.
5. **If we relist, what happens to my days on market and price history?** Ask what buyers will see.

Questions about your contract or your rights go to a real estate attorney.
