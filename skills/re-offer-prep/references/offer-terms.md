## Disclosures

Ask the listing agent for the seller's signed and initialed copies before the buyer signs, so Para 11 can say "has".

Checked this session:
- 765 ILCS 77/15 exempts some sellers from the Illinois Residential Real Property Disclosure Report, including lenders or their successors who took title by deed in lieu, consent judgment, or judicial deed after a foreclosure sale; fiduciaries of estates, guardianships, conservatorships, and trusts (including land trusts); and transfers to or from a governmental entity. If the MLS shows one of these, expect "no disclosure provided" and note it.
- A secondary source says the report applies to 1 to 4 unit residential property. Not confirmed against the statute's definitions.

From Michael's existing deal defaults: Lead-Based Paint disclosure and EPA pamphlet are federally required for homes built before 1978. Radon disclosure and IEMA guidelines go with every residential deal.

Anything about whether a specific seller is exempt is a legal call. Flag it to Castle Law.
