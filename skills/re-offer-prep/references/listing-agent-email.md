## A. Before the offer: availability and disclosures

Subject: Offer coming | [Address] | Disclosures request

Hi [First name],

I represent [Buyer name / entity] and we are preparing an offer on [Address] (MLS [number]).

Before we submit, can you confirm:
1. The property is still available and no offer has been accepted.
2. Your instructions for offers: deadline, required earnest money, proof of funds or pre-approval, and where to send.

Please also send the seller's signed and initialed disclosures so my buyer can acknowledge them with the offer:
- Illinois Residential Real Property Disclosure Report
- Lead-Based Paint Disclosure, and confirmation the EPA pamphlet was provided
- Disclosure of Information on Radon Hazards, with the IEMA Radon Testing Guidelines

If the seller is exempt from any of these, just tell me which one.
